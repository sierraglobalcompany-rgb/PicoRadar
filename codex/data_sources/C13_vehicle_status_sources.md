Consulted: 2026-08-27

## Official Portals

- RUNT consulta por placa: https://www.runt.gov.co/actores/ciudadano/consulta-de-vehiculos-por-placa
- Ministerio de Transporte RUNT notice: https://mintransporte.gov.co/publicaciones/11445/wwwruntgovco-unico-portal-disponible-del-runt/
- Fasecolda Automoviles companias autorizadas: https://www.fasecolda.com/ramos/automoviles/companias-autorizadas/
- Bogota impuesto sobre vehiculos automotores: https://www.haciendabogota.gov.co/es/impuestos/impuesto-sobre-vehiculos-automotores
- Bogota calendario tributario 2026: https://www.haciendabogota.gov.co/es/noticias/calendario-tributario-2026-vuelve-el-pago-por-cuotas-del-impuesto-vehiculos-en-bogota
- Bogota pagos oficiales: https://www.haciendabogota.gov.co/es/sdh/pagos-bogota
- Antioquia impuesto vehicular: https://www.vehiculosantioquia.com.co/impuestosweb/

## Boundaries

- PicoRadar only opens official HTTPS portals in the external browser.
- PicoRadar does not scrape RUNT, Fasecolda, Bogota, or Antioquia pages.
- PicoRadar does not store document IDs, CAPTCHA data, tax base, rates, sanctions, or tax estimates.
- SOAT and RTM status is derived only from the date saved locally by the user.
- Vehicle tax calendars are local guidance to reach official portals; unknown or stale jurisdictions ask users to verify on the official portal.
